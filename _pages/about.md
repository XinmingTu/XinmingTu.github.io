I am a **Founding Member of Technical Staff** at [Phylo](https://phylo.bio/) and a final-year PhD student at the [Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering](https://www.cs.washington.edu) at the University of Washington, advised by [Sara Mostafavi](http://saramostafavi.github.io).

My research centers on **agentic science**. I build rigorous benchmarks to understand how AI contributes to the research process and develop agentic architectures that make AI a better scientific collaborator—in a sense, “research about research.” Recent work includes [BenchGuard](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24955) (*COLM 2026*), [BiomniBench](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.12.724604v1), and [DrugDiscoveryBench](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/drugdiscoverybench).

I also work on **AI for regulatory genomics**, developing deep learning methods for sequence-to-function prediction and multi-omics integration. Representative work includes [SAGE-net](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.01.05.574421v1) (*Nature Methods*), [CLUE](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/662b1774ba8845fc1fa3d1fc0177ceeb-Abstract-Conference.html) (*NeurIPS Oral*), and [DeepAllele](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.04.09.648060v1) (*RECOMB 2026*).

Before UW, I studied Biology and Computer Science at Peking University. Please see my [CV](https://xinmingtu.cn/cv/) for more information.
