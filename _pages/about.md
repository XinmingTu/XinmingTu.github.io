I am a fifth year PhD student at the [Paul Allen School of Computer Science and Engineering](https://www.cs.washington.edu) at University of Washington, supervised by [Sara Mostafavi](http://saramostafavi.github.io). I am also a Member of Technical Staff at [Phylo](https://phylo.bio/). Before my PhD, I earned BSs in Biology and Computer Science from Peking University. During my undergraduate, I did some bioinformatics research at [Gao Lab](http://www.gao-lab.org/index.php/en/home/). Please see my [CV](https://xinmingtu.cn/cv/) for more information.

I am now working on **agentic science**—building rigorous benchmarks to understand how AI contributes to the research process ([BenchGuard](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24955), *arXiv 2026*; [BiomniBench](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.12.724604v1), *bioRxiv 2026*; [DrugDiscoveryBench](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/drugdiscoverybench), *Scale Labs preprint 2026*), and developing LLM-based agentic architectures to make AI agents better scientific collaborators. A kind of "research about research." I also work on **AI for regulatory genomics**, developing deep learning methods for sequence-to-function prediction and multi-omics integration ([CLUE](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/662b1774ba8845fc1fa3d1fc0177ceeb-Abstract-Conference.html), *NeurIPS 2022 Oral*; [SAGE-net](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.01.05.574421v1), *Nature Methods 2026*; [DeepAllele](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.04.09.648060v1), *RECOMB 2026*).
