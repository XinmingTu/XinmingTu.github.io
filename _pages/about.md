I am a fifth year PhD student at the [Paul Allen School of Computer Science and Engineering](https://www.cs.washington.edu) at University of Washington, supervised by [Sara Mostafavi](http://saramostafavi.github.io). Before my PhD, I earned BSs in Biology and Computer Science from Peking University. During my undergraduate, I did some bioinformatics research at [Gao Lab](http://www.gao-lab.org/index.php/en/home/). Please see my [CV](https://xinmingtu.cn/cv/) for more information.

I have recently started working on **AI systems for autonomous scientific reasoning and discovery**—building LLM-based agentic architectures and rigorous benchmarks to understand and enhance how AI can contribute to the research process, a kind of "research about research." I also work on **AI for regulatory genomics**, developing deep learning methods for sequence-to-function prediction and multi-omics integration, with work published at *NeurIPS 2022 Oral*, *Nature Methods 2026*, and *RECOMB 2026*.
